# Conviction

Conviction helps an analyst identify what is still unknown when evaluating a startup and decide what to ask next.

## Language

**Analyst**: The person reviewing the startup and deciding what information to request next.

**Case**: The evaluation of one startup, containing its assumptions, evidence and unresolved questions.

**Assumption**: A proposition whose truth matters to the evaluation.

**Evidence**: Information attributed to an identifiable source and used to assess an assumption. Evidence is not automatically verified fact.

**Open question**: Missing information relevant to an assumption.

**Next question**: The information request selected as the analyst's next step. It is not a recommendation to invest.
